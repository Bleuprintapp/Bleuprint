Kalena Gardner

Kalena Gardner is the founder of Bleuprint. She diagnoses why a business's marketing is not producing, names the bottleneck, and sequences the next move so the team can actually do it.

She works with founders and owners, especially local businesses that run on appointments, and currently with Ultrium (Mechanic OS).

She is based in Flint, Michigan. The company began in Detroit. Bleuprint LLC is a Michigan company, filed May 2026.
