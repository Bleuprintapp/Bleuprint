About

Kalena Gardner

Kalena Gardner is the founder of Bleuprint. She studies the evidence around a brand, identifies the constraint keeping it from being chosen, and sequences the next move so the founder can execute with confidence.

Her work crosses content strategy, brand development, creative direction, social content, podcast production, and website support. The diagnosis decides which of those skills the business actually needs—not the other way around.

When founders continue one-on-one, Kalena works beside them through weekly calls and hands-on implementation, with a strategy re-evaluation every month.

She is based in Flint, Michigan. The company began in Detroit. Bleuprint LLC is a Michigan company, filed May 2026.
