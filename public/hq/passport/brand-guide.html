2.0

the brand

Passport makes every AI agent identifiable, verifiable, and accountable before it is allowed to act. Everything in this guide serves that one sentence.

2.1 Positioning statement

For teams deploying AI agents into sensitive workflows, Passport is authorization and evidence infrastructure that binds an agent to an owner, a bounded capability, a policy decision, and verifiable proof, so automation can move quickly while action stays attached to human authority.

Short form for category conversations: the permission layer for agents.

2.2 Claims we never make

Generic IAM

Passport governs what an agent may do in a single consequential moment. Identity and access management covers a different scope, and equating them oversells us into the wrong bake-off.

Proof of humanity

Passport verifies an authorization decision. It makes no statement about whether a human being is real or present.

Unhackable systems

No claim of zero risk, ever. A receipt proves Passport's decision. It does not promise a downstream system executed the outcome.

2.3 Brand architecture
Logo and site
Your Passport

The full wordmark as it appears in the lockup, on yourpassport.ai and on social profiles.

Body copy and product

Legal and procurement

Contracts, investor documents, and vendor forms only. Keep it out of customer-facing copy.

2.4 The narrative in five beats
01
Trust

Agents now act. They do more than generate text.

02
Permission

Credentials and prompts leave the question of who authorized the action unanswered.

03
Scope

Permission has to be bounded by resource, action, value, risk, and time.

04
Proof

A durable, signed record has to remain after the action.

05
Passport

The model proposes. Passport decides. The organization can verify.

the logo

The mark is a scanning dial: twenty four segments around a fixed center, eight of them lit. It reads as a system checking something before it opens.

3.1 The mark
Primary. Paper and grey on dark ground.
Segments24
Angular pitch15.0°
Segment width / gap13.3° / 1.7°
Lit segments8, every third, at 45° intervals
Lit color#F9F9F8
Unlit color#575757
Segment band65% to 100% of radius
Inner ring44% of radius, hairline
Center dot17.6% of radius, #F9F9F8

On the site the dial is rendered live in code and rotates. Rebuild the motion from these values when it is needed in video. Exporting a single frame and animating that frame produces a different object.

3.2 The wordmark
YourPassport
Two words, set solid with no space between them. "Your" at Light 300, "Passport" at Bold 700, both in Paper.

The weight change carries the whole idea: the passport belongs to the person, and the thing that acts under it does not. Never set both words at the same weight, and never insert a space or a hyphen between them.

3.3 Lockups
YourPassport
Horizontal. Default everywhere.
Mark alone. Avatars, favicons, video stings.
YourPassport
On Paper. Ink wordmark, Ink and grey mark.

Gap between mark and wordmark equals the cap height of the letter P. Vertically center the mark on the wordmark's cap height, not on its baseline.

3.4 Clear space and minimum size

Clear space

Keep clear space equal to the mark's radius on all four sides of the lockup. Nothing enters that space: no type, no rules, no image edges, no contour lines.

Minimum size

Mark alone: 24 px on screen, 8 mm in print. Full lockup: 120 px wide on screen, 32 mm in print. Below 24 px the segment gaps close up and the dial reads as a solid ring.

24 px floor
3.5 Logo rules
✕Do not recolor the lit segments. They stay Paper on dark and Ink on light.
✕Do not add glow, shadow, bevel, or gradient to the mark.
✕Do not stretch, squash, or crop the dial to fit a container.
✕Do not redraw the segments by hand. Generate them from the values in 3.1.
✕Do not place the lockup on busy imagery without a solid scrim behind it.
✕Do not pair the mark with a second logo inside the clear space.

4.3 Hierarchy and ratio
Ground 70%
Paper 24%

Roughly seventy percent dark ground, a quarter Paper type, a thin band of gold structure, and a sliver of cyan. When cyan covers more than a headline, an icon, or a single line, it has stopped meaning anything.

Contrast: Paper on Deep Space passes AA at every size. Cyan on Deep Space passes for large text and interface elements. For cyan text under 18 px on Paper, darken to #0E6B80.

5.0

typography

One neo-grotesque carries the voice, and one monospace carries the evidence. The split is doing real work: anything that reads as a record is set in mono.

5.1 Overview
Aa

Inter

Display, headings, body, interface. Weights 300, 400, 600, 700. Tighten tracking to -0.02em at 24 px and above, and to -0.04em on display sizes.

Aa

IBM Plex Mono

Labels, receipts, identifiers, values, timestamps, code. Weights 400 and 500. Uppercase labels take 0.12em tracking.

Confirm against the live site before this is treated as final. Paris owns the implemented stack, and the wordmark may be drawn in a licensed grotesque rather than Inter.

Say
  • The model proposes. Passport decides.

  • Every sensitive action deserves a verifiable decision.
Avoid
  • Unhackable
  • Zero risk
  • Passport proves a human is real
  • Passport replaces IAM
6.3 The proof ladder

Every public claim carries one of these labels, stated plainly in the post or the asset. The labels never blur upward, and a claim never borrows credibility from the rung above it.

00Sourced reportPublished reporting or an official document from a third party. Name the outlet and the date in the asset itself, link the original, and quote regulatory text rather than paraphrasing it into a requirement. Used to establish the problem, never to imply a Passport result.
01ScenarioA hypothetical, labeled as hypothetical on the face of the asset.
02Lab demonstrationThe Passport team reproduced it internally. Say what was reproduced and in what environment.
03Design partner resultAn external team tested it outside production. Named only with their written approval.
04Customer caseProduction use with approved disclosure. The only rung that may carry a customer name.

Handling news

A news clip earns its place when the incident is documented and the outlet is named on screen. Keep the outlet name and date visible for the full time the footage runs, cut nothing that changes what the reporting said, and let the incident speak before Passport appears. The proof rule applies to the brand as much as to the product.

7.0

imagery

The visual world is the one built for the investor deck: deep space ground, a faint star field, thin gold contour lines, and one lit gate.

Contour lines bend around the gate. The gate is the only lit object in frame.

What the picture is saying

The contour lines are every path an agent could take. They run everywhere, unlabeled and unbounded. The gate is the one place a path narrows to a single opening, and the light marks the moment the decision is made. Read left to right, the frame moves from open ground to a bounded, accountable path.

✕More than one cyan focal point in a single frame.
✕Contour lines heavier than one pixel, or crossing in front of the gate.
✕People in default visuals. Show a person only when a specific human decision is the subject of the shot.

9.0

rules of the road

Five checks before anything leaves the building.

Every result claim carries its proof ladder label, and every news source carries its outlet and date.
A human approved this post. Automation drafts and schedules. It does not publish on its own during this sprint.
Customer facing copy says Passport. Legal and procurement copy says Passport, an Ultrium company.
Cyan appears once in the frame, on the thing that matters.
Nothing under stealth appears: founders, pipeline, non public architecture, unannounced partnerships.
9.1 Open decisions
Which dark and which cyan

Ink #181818 with Cyan #19B7DB from the strategy doc, or Deep Space #1A1E1F with Signal Cyan #3FD7DC from the shipped deck. One set wins and the other assets get rebuilt.

The typeface on record

Inter is the recommendation here. Paris confirms what the site actually loads and whether the wordmark is drawn in something licensed.

What gold is allowed to do

Contour Gold is live in the deck. Decide whether it is a permanent second accent or scenery that stays inside deck imagery.
